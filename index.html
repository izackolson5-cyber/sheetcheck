SheetCheck

Check a supplier spreadsheet against your Shopify export before you import it.

Why this exists: Shopify's own import docs warn that importing rows for an existing product with missing option columns can delete that product's variants. SheetCheck catches that - plus duplicate SKUs, broken prices, barcode problems and every price change - before you hit Import.

1 Drop in your files

Supplier spreadsheet (CSV) required

The new file from your supplier, before you touch Shopify.

Current Shopify export (CSV) recommended

Products → Export in your Shopify admin. Used to catch price changes and variant-deletion risk.

2 Map your columns

We guessed these from your headers. Fix any that are wrong - especially anything to do with Options, SKU and Price.

3 Results

Cleaned file: trims whitespace, fixes number formats, removes empty rows. Rows with errors are kept - fix or delete them in your spreadsheet app first.

Private by design

Your files never leave your browser. There is no upload, no server, no account, no tracking. Everything runs on this page, so your supplier pricing and store data stay yours. You can even load this page, go offline, and it still works.

How to use it

  1. In Shopify admin: Products → Export to get your current product CSV.
  2. Save your supplier's spreadsheet as CSV (Excel / Google Sheets: File → Download / Save As → CSV).
  3. Drop both in above, check the column mapping, run the check.
  4. Fix the errors in your file, then import into Shopify with confidence.
