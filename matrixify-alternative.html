A free Matrixify alternative for checking supplier CSVs

Let's be straight about this, because you came here to compare. Matrixify is the established, full-featured app for Shopify bulk imports, exports, updates and migrations. It is genuinely good, and if you run large scheduled imports or migrate a whole store, it is the right tool. SheetCheck is not that. It does one narrow job - checking a supplier spreadsheet against your store before you import it - and it does that job free.

What Matrixify does that SheetCheck doesn't

Scheduled and repeated imports, direct file imports into your store, exports to custom formats, migrations from other platforms, filtering and transforming data at scale, FTP and Google Sheets connections. It has a free Demo plan with tight limits, and paid plans start around US$20 a month. If your workflow is "import ten thousand products every week", pay for it - it's the professional option.

What SheetCheck does instead

SheetCheck doesn't import anything. You drop in the file a supplier sent you plus a current export from your Shopify store, and it tells you what would happen if you imported that file with Shopify's built-in CSV import: which products would change price or stock, which variants are at risk of being deleted (a documented Shopify behaviour when option columns are missing), which SKUs appear twice, which prices aren't numbers, which barcodes lost their leading zeros. Exact rows, plain English. Then you fix the file and import it yourself through Shopify's free built-in importer.

It runs entirely in your browser - the files never leave your computer, there's no account, and nothing is stored. It is brand new, so treat it as a second pair of eyes, not a guarantee.

Which one do you need

Choose Matrixify if you import regularly at scale, need scheduling, transformations or migrations, or want the import itself handled for you.
Choose SheetCheck if your situation is "a supplier emailed me a spreadsheet and I don't want it to wreck my store" - especially if you were about to do the import with Shopify's built-in CSV importer anyway. The check is free and takes about two minutes.

Honest summary: Matrixify is a toolbox; SheetCheck is the pre-flight checklist. Most "Matrixify alternative" searches from small merchants really need the checklist.
